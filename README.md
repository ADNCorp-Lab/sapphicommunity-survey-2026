# Community Survey — 2026

Landing page for a community research study exploring the digital needs,
experiences, and expectations of queer women across Latin America.

## About

This is a single-page site designed to reach participants in three languages
(Spanish, Portuguese, and English) and direct them to the corresponding survey form.
The study is anonymous and requires no registration.

## Structure

- `index.html` — self-contained page (HTML, CSS, JS in one file)
- No frameworks, no dependencies, no tracking

## Deployment

Hosted via GitHub Pages. No build step required.

## Privacy

No personal data is collected through this site.
Survey responses are handled by Google Forms under their privacy policy.
